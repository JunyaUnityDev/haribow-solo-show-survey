We decided not to stream this show

HARIBOW is doing its first solo theater show in Tokyo on Sept 29, 2026, at 7:30 PM Japan time. This page used to ask whether an online livestream would be worth setting up. We have decided not to stream the show — there will be no live stream and no archive, so the survey is closed. Thank you to everyone who answered.

If you bought a stream ticket, you are being refunded in full; you do not need to do anything. The show itself goes ahead as planned. Seats at the venue are on sale at ticket.haribow.com (the seat-picker and checkout are in Japanese). Questions: info@haribow.com. Learn more at our official site.
